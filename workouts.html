HOW TO STRUCTURE YOUR ROUTINE

3-DAY FULL BODY

Ideal for athletes balancing heavy work weeks. Focuses on squat, bench, deadlift compound movements with 48 hours recovery between sessions.

Frequency: Mon / Wed / Fri
4-DAY UPPER / LOWER

The optimal sweet spot for natural hypertrophy and strength. Hits each muscle group twice weekly with dedicated volume and intensity days.

Frequency: Mon / Tue & Thu / Fri
6-DAY PUSH / PULL / LEGS

High-volume bodybuilding & athletic split for advanced lifters. Requires strict adherence to calorie surplus and 8+ hours sleep.

Frequency: 6 Days On / 1 Day Rest
